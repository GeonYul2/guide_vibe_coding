# Retrieval and Memory Governance: customer-email-reply-agent

## Source Hierarchy

1. Current approved company policy documents.
2. User-provided customer email and permitted CRM metadata.
3. Historical approved reply examples after privacy review.
4. Model prior knowledge only for language style, never for policy facts.

## Freshness and Citation

Policy snippets must include document id, title, retrieved timestamp, and summary. Cached policy summaries expire after 24 hours or immediately when policy index version changes. Routine replies require at least one policy citation.

## Retention

Session memory stores only redacted message summaries and decision traces. Raw customer emails and sensitive identifiers are not stored in long-term agent memory.
